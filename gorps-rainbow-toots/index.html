Gorp is hungry.

Catch falling trash. Every snack has a color. Toot out the colors as a rainbow, or plop them out as a swirly rainbow poop.

Three in a row of the rainbow (like red, orange, yellow) scores the most. Don't let 7 pieces of litter pile up.

Move with ← → or A D, or drag on the game. Toot needs 1+ snacks and gives Gorp a speed boost. Plop needs a full belly and buries nearby litter.
